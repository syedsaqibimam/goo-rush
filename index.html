GOO RUSH

Zombie Parkour
📖 How to play
🚁 Survive 2 minutes, then reach the rescue helicopter to escape!
Run. The horde never stops. Jump at walls to climb onto rooftops, slide to dodge grabs.
🧱 Wall run: jump at a wall and keep pushing. Push into it to run UP, along it to run across. Jump again to wall-jump!
Zombies use ramps to follow you up. Strong ones climb walls!
Ziplines on tall roofs: zombies can't follow you.
You start with a knife. Open chests for guns and ammo:
🟫 Pistol (common) · 🟦 Minigun (uncommon) · 🟨 Alien Blaster (rare)
🟢 Normal · 🔵 Fast · 🟠 Strong — they all melt into goo.
Tap the map (top-left) to make it big.
🪤 Cat & mouse traps! Traps are hidden around the city. Lure zombies into them: 🍌 banana (slip!), 🔨 anvil (CLANG!), 🌀 spring (BOING!). They're on your map.
